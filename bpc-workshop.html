About the event

The IEEE MTT-S Broadening Participation Committee (BPC) brings its workshop to Montevideo on December 7–8, 2026, fostering collaboration and opportunities for students and young professionals across Latin America.

Keynotes

MTT-S BPC workshop will include talks by international delegates on the research topics in RF to THz technologies, systems & applications. The delegates will be available for discussions with the students and faculties.

Student competitions

Proposed activities include a Three Minute Presentation Competition (3MPC) and a proposal writing workshop & competition. Final program, rules and registration details will be announced.

Career guidance & mentoring sessions

Insightful discussion on various career paths that can be pursued as RF engineer, showcasing the importance and impacts of MTT-S technologies in the society and guidance on how to make the most out of the engineering career in this field.

Proposal Writing Competition

The Proposal Writing Competition is an interactive hands-on workshop where participants work in collaborative teams of three. This interactive workshop will outline strategies for paper acceptance at MTT-S sponsored conferences and journals, and discuss requirements for conference papers expansion into journal publications.

This activity is particularly relevant for scholarship applications, research funding applications, project planning, and impact communication.

Why participate?

  • Learn how to write and work in teams to build a 1-page research proposal summary.
  • Proposal summary includes objective, problem statement, methodology, and anticipated outcomes.
  • The best research summaries will be selected for awards.

Career Passport Mentoring

Career Passport is a 90-minute mentoring session at the IEEE MTT-S BPC Workshop in Montevideo, where students and early-career engineers rotate through 4 small tables hosted by senior MTT-S members and collect a stamp at each one.

Mentors stamp the passport only after a real question, so every stop turns into an actual conversation. We close with one-sentence takeaways from attendees, a wisdom wall, a raffle for full passports and a group photo. The idea is simple: give participants, many of them from smaller universities and attending an IEEE event for the first time, an easy way to talk face to face with people they usually only see on stage.

The 4 tables

  • Grad school & scholarships
  • Careers in RF industry
  • Getting involved in MTT-S
  • Global careers from Latin America

About IEEE MTT-S

IEEE Microwave Theory & Technology Society (MTT-S) advances microwave theory and its applications, from radio frequency to millimeter-wave and terahertz technologies. Through its Broadening Participation Committee (BPC), MTT-S promotes inclusive access to technical education, professional development and research opportunities.

The December 2026 workshop in Montevideo, Uruguay, brings together a regional community to share knowledge, exchange ideas and encourage future careers in RF and microwave engineering.

Organizing university

Universidad Católica del Uruguay (UCU) is the participating university in the Montevideo edition of the IEEE MTT-S BPC Workshop.

Universidad Católica del Uruguay (UCU) brings academic experience in engineering, research and education, connecting students and faculty with the workshop.

About Montevideo

Montevideo, the capital of Uruguay, stretches along the Río de la Plata. Its waterfront, historic neighborhoods, cultural venues and universities make it a welcoming destination for students, academics and visitors.

Plaza Independencia is one of Montevideo's most recognizable public squares. It is home to the monument to José Artigas and surrounded by landmarks including Palacio Salvo, Palacio Estévez and the nearby Teatro Solís, which opened in 1856.

Visitors can explore Ciudad Vieja, walk along the Rambla facing the Río de la Plata and discover the city's music, architecture and cultural traditions. Montevideo offers an inviting setting for an international engineering workshop.
