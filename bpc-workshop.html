Why should you join us in this workshop?

Join students, researchers and professionals in Montevideo for the IEEE MTT-S Broadening Participation Workshop. Explore RF, microwave, millimeter-wave and THz technologies through technical talks, interactive activities and opportunities to connect with international experts.

The December 2026 workshop in Montevideo, Uruguay, brings together a regional community to share knowledge, exchange ideas and encourage future careers in RF and microwave engineering.

The workshop aims to bridge theory and practice, encourage student participation and foster collaboration among academic communities in Uruguay and across Latin America.

About the event

The IEEE MTT-S Broadening Participation Committee (BPC) brings its workshop to Montevideo on December 7–8, 2026, fostering collaboration and opportunities for students and young professionals across Latin America.

Proposal Writing Competition (PWC)

The Proposal Writing Competition is an interactive hands-on workshop where participants work in collaborative teams of three. This interactive workshop will outline strategies for paper acceptance at MTT-S sponsored conferences and journals, and discuss requirements for conference papers expansion into journal publications.

This activity is particularly relevant for scholarship applications, research funding applications, project planning, and impact communication.

Why participate?

  • Learn how to write and work in teams to build a 1-page research proposal summary.
  • Proposal summary includes objective, problem statement, methodology, and anticipated outcomes.
  • The best research summaries will be selected for awards.

Career Passport Mentoring

Career Passport is a 90-minute mentoring session at the IEEE MTT-S BPC Workshop in Montevideo, where students and early-career engineers rotate through 4 small tables hosted by senior MTT-S members and collect a stamp at each one.

Mentors stamp the passport only after a real question, so every stop turns into an actual conversation. We close with one-sentence takeaways from attendees, a wisdom wall, a raffle for full passports and a group photo. The idea is simple: give participants, many of them from smaller universities and attending an IEEE event for the first time, an easy way to talk face to face with people they usually only see on stage.

The 4 tables

  • Grad school & scholarships
  • Careers in RF industry
  • Getting involved in MTT-S
  • Global careers from Latin America

Speed Mentoring

Speed Mentoring is an interactive mentoring activity at the IEEE MTT-S BPC Workshop in Montevideo, designed to connect students and early-career engineers with experienced professionals and leaders in the microwave and RF community.

Participants rotate through a series of short mentoring sessions, each hosted by a different senior MTT-S member or invited professional. At each stop, they have the opportunity to introduce themselves, ask questions, seek career advice, and learn from the mentor's personal and professional experiences.

The rotations create an informal and dynamic environment where participants can interact with several mentors in a limited amount of time, gaining different perspectives on career development, research, leadership, industry opportunities, and professional growth.

About the organizers

Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society MTT-S Broadening Participation Committee

IEEE Microwave Theory & Technology Society (MTT-S) advances microwave theory and its applications, from radio frequency to millimeter-wave and terahertz technologies. Through its Broadening Participation Committee (BPC), MTT-S promotes inclusive access to technical education, professional development and research opportunities. Learn more about IEEE MTT-S ↗

Organizing university

Universidad Católica del Uruguay

Universidad Católica del Uruguay (UCU) is one of the two organizing universities of the IEEE MTT-S BPC Workshop 2026 in Montevideo. It promotes education, innovation and research in engineering and technology, fostering collaboration between students, faculty and industry professionals. Its participation in the workshop encourages knowledge exchange and professional development in RF, microwave and telecommunications technologies.

About Montevideo

Montevideo, the capital of Uruguay, stretches along the Río de la Plata. Its waterfront, historic neighborhoods, cultural venues and universities make it a welcoming destination for students, academics and visitors.

Plaza Independencia is one of Montevideo's most recognizable public squares. It is home to the monument to José Artigas and surrounded by landmarks including Palacio Salvo, Palacio Estévez and the nearby Teatro Solís, which opened in 1856.

Visitors can explore Ciudad Vieja, walk along the Rambla facing the Río de la Plata and discover the city's music, architecture and cultural traditions. Montevideo offers an inviting setting for an international engineering workshop.

Travel & safety tips

Enjoy your stay in Montevideo! Here are some practical recommendations for a safe and pleasant visit.

Safe mobility

Use licensed taxis or trusted ride-hailing apps. For public transportation, use the Cómo Ir app to check bus routes and schedules. Plan your trips in advance.

Personal belongings

Keep your personal belongings secure, especially in crowded areas. Avoid leaving valuable items unattended and keep a digital copy of your passport.

Exploring Montevideo

Stay aware of your surroundings, especially in busy tourist areas. Use well-lit streets at night and apply sunscreen when spending time outdoors during December.

Emergency contacts

In case of an emergency, dial 911. Keep your travel insurance information available and contact the workshop organizing team if you need assistance during your stay.
